# Orders and delivery

## Where we ship

We ship to Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands and Portugal. We don't ship outside the European Union yet.

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 2 pm leave our roastery the same day. Delivery takes two to four working days. Express delivery arrives the next working day in Germany and the Netherlands.

## Tracking your order

You get a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the roastery. You can also find it under Orders in your account.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order until it leaves the roastery: open Orders in your account and choose Change or Cancel. After that, you can return it instead.
