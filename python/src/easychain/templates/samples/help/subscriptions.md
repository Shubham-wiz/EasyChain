# Coffee subscriptions

## How subscriptions work

Pick a coffee and how often you want it: every two, four or six weeks. You pay per delivery and shipping is free.

## Pausing or skipping

You can pause your subscription or skip a delivery any time before the next roast date, under Subscriptions in your account.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time under Subscriptions. There's no notice period and no fee; deliveries that already left the roastery still arrive.
