# Payments and invoices

## Ways to pay

We take credit and debit cards, PayPal, iDEAL in the Netherlands, and bank transfer for business accounts.

## Invoices

Every order comes with an invoice by email. You can also download invoices under Orders in your account. Business customers can add their VAT number in Settings.

## Charged twice?

A second charge is usually a card check that disappears within three days. If it is still there after three days, write to support with the order number and we refund it the same day.
