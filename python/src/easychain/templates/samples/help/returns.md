# Returns and refunds

## Returning an item

You can return unopened items within 30 days of delivery. Start a return under Orders in your account and print the free return label. Opened coffee and tea can't be returned, unless they arrived damaged.

## Refunds

We refund to the way you paid. Refunds take five working days after your return reaches us. Gift cards are refunded as store credit.

## Damaged or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, send us a photo within 14 days. We send a replacement straight away, and you don't need to return the item.
