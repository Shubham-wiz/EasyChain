# Your account

## Resetting your password

Open Settings, choose Reset password and follow the link we email you. The link works for one hour. If the email doesn't arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings, then Profile, and type the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address; the change takes effect once you click it.

## Two-step sign-in

Turn on two-step sign-in under Settings, then Security. You can use an authenticator app or text messages. Keep the backup codes somewhere safe.

## Deleting your account

Write to support@beanandleaf.example from the address on your account. We delete your account and your data within thirty days. Orders that are still on their way are delivered first.
